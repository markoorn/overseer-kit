# Brand voice

Read by `ad-brief` steps 1, 5 and 6. The voice check compares every draft headline against
the real examples here, so the examples matter more than the adjectives. Fill the example
sections with lines that have actually run or been approved.

## Who we talk to

The two or three audiences that want genuinely different ads, one line each on what they
care about and what they would roll their eyes at.

- <Audience 1>: <...>
- <Audience 2>: <...>

## Registers

Most brands run two registers at once in a single post, and collapsing them is the usual
reason a draft reads flat.

- **In-image (headline and subhead):** <declarative, punchy, all caps or sentence case,
  punctuation that serves the idea>
- **Caption:** <conversational, self-deprecating, asks things, running bits>

## On-voice headlines (real ones)

Three to five lines that have run or been approved. These are the benchmark.

1. <...>
2. <...>
3. <...>

## Off-voice headlines (and why)

Three lines that were cut, each with the reason, so the same mistake is not re-drafted.

1. <line> — <reason: generic, explains the joke, belittles the audience, ...>
2. <...>
3. <...>

## Rules

- Self-deprecation targets the brand, never the audience.
- Never say the product is an app as filler ("in the app"). Name the thing itself.
- <Local specificity: currency, slang, place names, and whether it is a pillar>
- <Puns: welcome or not>
- <Banned words and claims, one per line>
- Numbers: never a number that has not been checked against the code, the store or the
  campaign record. How many, how often and through which channel are operational facts to
  ask about, not infer.

## Standing hashtags

The tags every post starts from, then post-specific ones are added, broadest first, six to
ten in total.

```
#<brand> #<category> #<market>
```

Tags to avoid and why: <e.g. the market split across three forms of one tag; the one
chosen>.

## Running bits

Recurring characters, phrases or jokes the audience recognises, with the rule for using
each (how often, in which register).
